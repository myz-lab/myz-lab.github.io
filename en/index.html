Image processing
that supports people

The Image Information Processing Systems Lab (Miyazaki Lab) combines image processing and artificial intelligence to build systems for communication support and everyday social challenges.

RESEARCH

Research Topics

Reading words from lip movements

Machine lip-reading and speech video generation to support communication between people with hearing impairments and hearing people. Our lip-reading training app for iPhone and iPad, Dokuwa Club, is publicly available.

Recognition technology for daily life

Real-time object detection and tracking applied to practical problems such as automated waste sorting.

Treating sound as an image

Sound is converted into spectrogram images so that image-processing AI can remove unwanted noise from audio and video.
